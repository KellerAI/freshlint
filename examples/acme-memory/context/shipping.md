# Shipping

## Carriers
<!-- memory: verified=2026-07-02 review_by=2026-10-02 -->
- Kits ship with two regional ground carriers, Parcel Owl and Desert Freight.
- Igniters are restricted cargo: ground shipping only, never air.

## Rates
<!-- memory: verified=2026-09-10 review_by=2026-12-10 -->

- Flat 12 USD per kit inside the home region, 25 USD elsewhere.
- Free shipping on club orders of ten kits or more.
